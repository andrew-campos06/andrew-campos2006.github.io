CAD Project

Computer Graphics and Design Class Project | New York, NY | June - September 2026

Modeled a toy airplane in SolidWorks from measurements and reference photos, using symmetry, lofted surfaces, splines, guide curves, fillets, and split features to capture its aerodynamic form and injection-molded details. Built dimensioned components and assembled them into a cohesive CAD model while managing complex curvature, fit, and drawing constraints.
